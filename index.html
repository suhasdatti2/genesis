Our story

Born from a belief that the web can feel alive.

Genesis LP started in a single room with three engineers and one stubborn idea: most of the web is slow, forgettable and built for machines instead of people. We set out to change that.

Today we're a distributed studio of designers and engineers who treat performance as a feature and motion as a language. Every project is a chance to prove the web can be as fluid as native — and far more beautiful.

  • Design and engineering under one roof
  • Obsessive about performance budgets
  • Accessibility is never an afterthought
